## Acknowledgements

- `coffee` grew out of a single-file script of the same name. The package keeps
  that script's display exactly — see the parity suite in
  [`tests/test_parity.py`](tests/test_parity.py).
- The terminal rendering is all [Rich](https://github.com/Textualize/rich) by
  Will McGugan and contributors, with help text from
  [rich-argparse-plus](https://github.com/michelcrypt4d4mus/rich-argparse-plus).
- Process and battery data come from
  [psutil](https://github.com/giampaolo/psutil) by Giampaolo Rodolà.
- The glyphs are [Nerd Fonts](https://www.nerdfonts.com/) codepoints.

## Provenance

Parts of this codebase were written with
